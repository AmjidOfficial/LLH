CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Legacy Leathers may receive information you choose to provide when you send a product enquiry, request a bespoke jacket, submit a review or place an order request. This can include your name, phone number, email address, delivery details and the product or service you asked about.

How we use information

We use enquiry and order details to reply to you, check product requirements, prepare quotations, confirm delivery and manage customer support. Reviews and comments are checked before they are displayed publicly. Do not send payment card details or passwords through WhatsApp or public forms.

Your choices

You may contact the atelier to ask about information you have shared or to request an update. We do not ask customers to create an account just to browse the public catalogue.
