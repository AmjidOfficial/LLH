SHOPPING INFORMATION

Terms of enquiry.

The Legacy Leathers website presents jacket styles and information from the brand catalogue. A catalogue picture describes a style and does not confirm that a physical item is currently in stock.

Prices and availability

Prices, available sizes, colours, materials, ready stock and made-to-order options must be confirmed by the atelier before an order is accepted. A product enquiry or WhatsApp message is not, by itself, a confirmed order.

Bespoke requests

Custom jacket requests are reviewed by the atelier. The final specifications, measurements, quotation, production timing and delivery arrangements are confirmed with the customer before work begins.

Orders and delivery

An online order request is subject to stock, payment and delivery confirmation by the store. Delivery charges and any applicable terms are shared before dispatch. Payment is not considered complete until the store or payment provider confirms receipt.
